## Advisory Capabilities

The create-ticket orchestrator never hands you Advisory entries, so the speculative-invocation ban in `## Side-effect ban` applies in full. The `**Advisory consultation**:` field of your Result envelope is optional; when you write it, its value is `(none)`.
